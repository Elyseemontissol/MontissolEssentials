Montissol Essentials is inviting residents in and around Manhattan, Kansas, to express interest in custodial services work at the KS019 Army Reserve facility (715 Griffth Drive).

Custodial work is performed during normal duty hours, generally Monday through Friday, 7:30 AM to 4:00 PM local time, excluding federal holidays. We are looking for dependable custodians and an on-site supervisor / point of contact. Uniforms and ID badges are provided, and all personnel are required to complete an NCIC-III background check, AT Level I awareness training, OPSEC Level I awareness training, and iWATCH training within 30 days of start. E-Verify eligibility is required.
