Support the National Weather Service in Melbourne

Montissol Essentials is inviting residents in and around Melbourne, Florida, to express interest in two janitorial positions supporting the National Weather Service office at 421 Croton Road — one full-time role and one backup role to cover time off and busy weeks.

Work is performed in the evenings, Monday through Saturday, between 5:00 PM and 9:00 PM local time. The 4,500 sq ft facility includes offices, restrooms, a kitchen, and a computer-equipment room that requires specific damp-mop-only care. All required cleaning supplies must be environmentally friendly, unscented, non-toxic, and biodegradable. A Low Risk security background investigation and standard OSHA compliance are required before starting on site.
