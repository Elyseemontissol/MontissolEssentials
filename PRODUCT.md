# Montissol Essentials LLC

## What it is

Facility-services contractor delivering janitorial, industrial cleaning, grounds, transportation, and support staffing to federal and commercial sites. Primary surface is `montissolessentials.com`, a marketing + capability-statement site that doubles as a hiring + bidding hub for the business.

## Who buys

**Primary — Contracting Officers (CO) and Contracting Officers' Representatives (COR)** at federal agencies evaluating vendors for facility-services solicitations (SAM.gov, agency portals, FedBizOpps). They need: eligibility (CAGE, UEI, NAICS), proof of past performance, quality-control discipline documented, and a human to call. They decide in minutes, often from a vendor's homepage after a SAM.gov search.

**Secondary — Facility managers at commercial sites** evaluating replacement for an incumbent janitorial/cleaning provider. They need: service breadth, reliability signals, local coverage, and clear next-step contact.

**Tertiary — Job seekers** applying to posted roles (the site also hosts the hiring agent + applications).

## What the homepage must do

For a CO landing from SAM.gov: answer in 15 seconds whether this vendor is eligible, credible, and worth a quote. Specifically:
1. Prove eligibility (CAGE / UEI / NAICS visible above the fold)
2. Show past-performance evidence (named contracts, named agencies — Army, VA, NWS, USACE)
3. Signal QC discipline (not just claim it)
4. Give an unambiguous next step (quote request OR direct contact)

For a commercial facility manager: establish service breadth + reliability without the federal framing becoming disqualifying.
